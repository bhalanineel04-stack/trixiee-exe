POSTCARD 0003 · NEVER SENT BY POST

Dear Pragyna,

There are a thousand beautiful things in the world that don't ask to be noticed. The moon doesn't ask the sea to glow. The mountains don't ask the clouds to stay. They just keep being what they are—and somehow, that's enough to make a person stop and look.


I think there is something lovely about people like that, too.

Keep this somewhere in the back of your mind: you don't have to become more extraordinary to be worth appreciating. You already bring your own kind of light. Even on the days you can't see it yourself.

03 / UNFOLD

One thing the
night asked me to say.

Not a grand confession. Not a question you have to answer. Just something honest, from one human to another.

Hey Trixiee, I wanted to make you something you wouldn't just read and forget in three seconds. Something with little corners to explore, because the people who matter to us deserve more than a copy-paste message. I don't know the perfect word for every kind of bond, and maybe we don't need to force one onto ours. I just know I'm glad you're in my world. I like that you're you — not some polished, always-happy version of you, but the real person who has good days, strange days, quiet days, and days when everything feels like too much. I hope life gives you places that feel like moonlight on water. People around whom you can exhale. Mountains that remind you how far you've come. Rain that washes the noise away. And, when the world feels dim, a small unexpected glow that reminds you there is still wonder here. And if this little page gave you even one real smile, then the hours spent making it were worth it. No pressure to say anything perfect back. No hidden test. Just a little piece of care, left here for you to find. — Neel. Your Lambo. ☾
