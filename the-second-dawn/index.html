FOR PRAGYNA · WITH CARE, ALWAYS
A NOTE FROM THE WESTERN GARDEN ✧

Dear Pragyna,

I hope life gives you more than beautiful moments. I hope it gives you the kind of peace that stays after the music ends, the kind of happiness that doesn't need to be explained, and the courage to walk away from anything that asks you to become less than yourself.

May you never mistake being wanted for being valued. May you never wait for permission to take up space. And when life turns a page you didn't expect, may you meet it with the quiet confidence of someone who knows her story is far from over.

There are a thousand ways to describe a queen. But my favourite is simply this: someone who keeps her heart kind without surrendering her strength.

I hope your next chapters are extraordinary.

With warmth,
Neel(Lambo)
